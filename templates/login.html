How It Works

From consultation to placement in three easy steps

1

Upload or Enter Client Details

Upload a voice recording of your client consultation or manually enter details including care level needs (Independent, Assisted, Memory Care), budget range, timeline, and special requirements like pets or couples accommodations.

2

AI Analyzes & Matches

Our AI processes the information, transcribes voice recordings, extracts key requirements, and searches the database for communities that best match your client's unique needs and preferences.

3

Review Ranked Recommendations

Receive a ranked list of community recommendations with match scores, pricing, availability, and detailed explanations of why each community fits your client's requirements.
