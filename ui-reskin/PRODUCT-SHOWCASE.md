# Senior Living Placement AI
## Intelligent Community Matching for Families & Placement Agents

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Finding the right senior living community is **overwhelming**:
- 100+ communities to evaluate
- Complex care requirements (Independent, Assisted, Memory Care)
- Budget constraints and hidden costs
- Location preferences and family proximity
- Timeline pressures (immediate vs. flexible)

**Traditional process:** 30 minutes of manual research per client
**Our solution:** 15 seconds with AI-powered recommendations

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. **Voice-First AI Agent**
- Real-time conversation with Gemini AI
- Natural language understanding
- Speaks recommendations aloud
- QR code access for events/kiosks

### 2. **Audio Consultation Processing**
- Upload recorded client calls
- Automatic transcription & extraction
- Works in English, Hindi, Spanish

### 3. **8-Dimension AI Ranking Engine**
| Dimension | What It Evaluates |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Business Value | Partner status, commission rates |
| Total Cost | Monthly fees + upfront costs |
| Geographic Distance | Real distance from client location |
| Availability | Waitlist times vs. timeline needs |
| Budget Efficiency | Value for money analysis |
| Couple Friendliness | Options for couples |
| Amenity Match | Services vs. special needs |
| Holistic Fit | AI-powered overall assessment |

### 4. **CRM Integration**
- Automatic Google Sheets sync
- Consultation history tracking
- Performance analytics dashboard
